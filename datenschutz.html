Visiting this website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Technical connection data is sent to GitHub to deliver the requested page. GitHub explains that visitors' IP addresses in particular are logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub receives this data. Our legitimate interest in providing and securing the website is the legal basis under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The website cannot be delivered without the technically necessary connection data.

GitHub may also process personal data in the United States and other countries. According to GitHub's privacy statement, it generally relies on standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for international transfers. Information about how long GitHub retains security logs is governed by GitHub's statements; GitHub does not provide a fixed period specifically for GitHub Pages.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not embed its own analytics or marketing services. The page files do not set their own cookies or access your browser's storage.

Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the contact details and message you provide to handle and answer your enquiry. Providing these details is voluntary; without the information needed to reply, we may be unable to deal with your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual or contractual matters, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The mailbox info@heratec.de is hosted by STRATO GmbH. STRATO processes the data needed to provide email; telecommunications providers involved may process data relating to phone calls. See STRATO's privacy notice for details. We delete enquiries once they have been dealt with and no legal retention duty or legitimate reason requires further storage.

External links

Dealer links lead to independent websites. Their operators' privacy notices apply to data processed there.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Contact us at the email address above to exercise your rights. This website does not use automated decision-making or profiling.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For private companies based in Lower Saxony, the State Commissioner for Data Protection in Lower Saxony is responsible.
