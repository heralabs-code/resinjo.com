About ReSinJo

ReSinJo is the shelving systems brand of HERATEC Export u. Beteiligungsges. mbH for retail, warehousing and commercial use. The range covers solutions for clear product presentation in stores and organised storage in business premises.

Whether you are fitting out a store, adding storage capacity or extending an existing rack, the right choice depends on dimensions, load requirements and matching components. Check the specifications of each product before ordering.

Our Range

From shop floor to warehouse: discover shelving types for different goods, spaces and workflows.

Retail Shelving

Display shelving for organised retail merchandising. Depending on the system, shelves, back panels and accessories can be selected to suit the sales floor.

Warehouse Shelving

Shelving for stock, cartons and work materials, keeping storage spaces tidy and goods easy to reach.

Shelving Units

Shelving units for small parts, archives and manually picked goods. Dimensions and shelf loads vary by model.

Pallet Racking

Pallet racking creates organised positions for palletised goods. Pallet sizes, loads and site conditions all matter when planning.

Heavy-Duty Racking

Heavy-duty racking comes in different configurations for larger and heavier goods. Always check the stated shelf and bay loads.

Quality & Compatibility

ReSinJo focuses on functional shelving systems for commercial use. To compare a specific rack with a well-known brand, check its material, finish, dimensions and stated load ratings.

Some retail shelving components may fit common systems, including those by Tegometall. Before extending a rack, check the product series, pitch, connections and load information. Compatibility and equivalent quality cannot be assumed for every component.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are ReSinJo racks as good as established brands?

Whether a rack is equally suitable depends on the specific model and application. Compare dimensions, material, workmanship and stated shelf and bay loads. A general claim of equivalent quality for every product would require product-specific evidence.

Do ReSinJo retail shelving parts fit Tegometall?

Some components may fit common systems. The fit depends on the series, pitch and connection. Compare specifications or ask the dealer before purchasing.

Which shelving type should I choose?

Retail shelving presents goods in stores; shelving units suit manually picked small items; pallet racking stores palletised goods. For heavy loads, also check the specified ratings and site requirements.

Where can I buy shelving and components?

The dealers above offer different ranges: used fittings and warehouse equipment at Heratec.de, new retail shelving and components at Ladenregal.shop, and pallet and other warehouse racking at Palettenregal.net.
